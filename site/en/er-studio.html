ER studio

Draw ER and EER diagrams, map them to tables automatically and create the tables in MySQL; or write your own tables and turn them back into an ER diagram. There and back in one place.

1 · Diagram

Start from an example or draw your own on an empty diagram. Click items to edit them in the panel on the right, drag them to place them. Your work is saved in this browser.

2 · ER → Tables

The diagram above is mapped to tables with the book's Ch 9 mapping algorithm (Elmasri & Navathe 9.1–9.2). The result follows the diagram as you change it. Every step says which rule was applied and why.

3 · Tables → ER

Write your own CREATE TABLE statements (with PRIMARY KEY, FOREIGN KEY and UNIQUE constraints). The tables are created in a separate MySQL database, the constraints are read from information_schema, and an ER diagram is derived by running the mapping rules backwards. This is called reverse engineering.

4 · ER design exercises

Read the requirements, draw the diagram in the editor above and press Check. Your diagram is compared with the solution structurally: entities, weak entities, keys, kinds of attributes, cardinality and participation of relationships, specializations. Relationship names are free: relationships are matched by the entities they connect. Use the given names for entities and attributes.

Syntax

The text form of the diagram. Indented lines are the attributes of the entity or relationship above them.

entity EMPLOYEE                    -- a regular entity type
  Ssn key                          -- key attribute (underlined)
  Name(Fname, Minit, Lname)        -- composite attribute
  Locations multi                  -- multivalued (double ellipse)
  Age derived                      -- derived (dashed ellipse)

weak entity DEPENDENT              -- weak entity (double rectangle)
  Dependent_name partial           -- partial key (dashed underline)

relationship WORKS_FOR: EMPLOYEE N total, DEPARTMENT 1 total   -- N:1, both total (double lines)
relationship WORKS_FOR: EMPLOYEE (1,1), DEPARTMENT (4,N)       -- the same with (min,max)
  Hours                                                        -- relationship attribute
identifying relationship DEPENDENTS_OF: EMPLOYEE 1, DEPENDENT N total
relationship SUPERVISION: EMPLOYEE 1 role=supervisor, EMPLOYEE N role=supervisee   -- recursive
relationship SUPPLY: SUPPLIER M, PROJECT N, PART P                                 -- ternary

specialization EMPLOYEE -> SECRETARY, ENGINEER disjoint partial on Job_type   -- d / o, total / partial
specialization EMPLOYEE -> MANAGER                                            -- one subclass
category OWNER = PERSON | BANK | COMPANY partial                              -- union type
  • Cardinality: 1, N, M (any letter means "many"). Without total the participation is partial.
  • If you write (min,max), the 1/N labels and total/partial are computed from it. The Notation selector under the diagram switches between the two notations.
  • Subclasses and categories not written as an entity are added as entities without attributes.
  • Parts starting with # or -- are comments.
